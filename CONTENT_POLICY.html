Overview

This Content Policy governs the posting by Members of content on the kicknap Platform ("Content"). Content includes text, photos, videos, reviews, messages, and any other material you share on the Platform. By posting Content, you agree to comply with this policy.

1. Content Standards

All Content on the Platform must:

  • Be accurate and truthful
  • Be relevant to the booking or space being described
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Comply with these Terms, our Community Guidelines, and our Nondiscrimination Policy
  • Be appropriate for a general audience

2. Prohibited Content

You may not post Content that:

  • Is illegal — Content that violates any applicable law or regulation
  • Is discriminatory — Content that discriminates against any individual or group based on race, religion, ethnicity, national origin, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or any other protected characteristic
  • Is hateful or violent — Content that promotes hatred, violence, terrorism, or extremism
  • Is sexually explicit — Content that is pornographic, sexually explicit, or exploitative
  • Is harassing or bullying — Content that harasses, bullies, or intimidates any individual
  • Is deceptive or misleading — Content that is false, fraudulent, or intended to deceive other Members
  • Infringes intellectual property — Content that violates copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property rights
  • Contains personal information — Content that discloses another person's personal information without their consent
  • Is spam or unsolicited advertising — Content that is primarily promotional, commercial, or repetitive
  • Contains malware — Content that contains viruses, malware, or other harmful code
  • Promotes illegal activities — Content that facilitates or encourages illegal activities, including drug use, prostitution, or human trafficking
  • Contains hidden cameras or recording devices — Content that promotes or facilitates hidden surveillance

3. Listing Content Standards

Listings must include:

  • Accurate photos that represent the current condition and appearance of the space
  • Honest descriptions of amenities, size, rules, and any limitations
  • Fair and transparent pricing
  • Accurate availability information

Listings may not include:

  • Photos that are digitally altered to misrepresent the space
  • Descriptions that are materially inaccurate
  • Hidden fees or charges not disclosed in the price breakdown
  • Content that discriminates against any group of people

4. Review Content Standards

Reviews must:

  • Be based on an actual booking and experience
  • Be honest and accurate
  • Be relevant to the experience

Reviews may not:

  • Be retaliatory (e.g., a negative review because the other party left a negative review)
  • Contain discriminatory language
  • Contain personal attacks or harassment
  • Be fraudulent (e.g., fake reviews, reviews for bookings that never occurred)

5. Content Moderation (DSA Compliance)

In compliance with the EU Digital Services Act (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065), kicknap:

  • Provides mechanisms for Members and third parties to notify us of allegedly illegal Content
  • Actively investigates and moderates Content that may be illegal or incompatible with our Terms
  • Provides statements of reasons when Content is removed or restricted
  • Provides an internal complaint handling system for content moderation decisions
  • Cooperates with designated trusted flaggers

5.1 Reporting Illegal Content

Members and third parties can report allegedly illegal Content by contacting content@kicknap.com. Reports should include:

  • A description of the Content in question
  • The reason it is allegedly illegal
  • The reporter's contact information
  • A statement of good faith belief that the Content is illegal

5.2 Automated Moderation

kicknap may use automated tools to identify, review, and moderate Content. If automated means have been used to review Content and action has been taken, we will notify the affected Member of the use of automated means, unless legitimate interests preclude such notice.

7. Enforcement

kicknap reserves the right to:

  • Remove Content that violates this policy
  • Restrict visibility of Content that is under review
  • Suspend or terminate accounts that repeatedly violate this policy
  • Report illegal Content to law enforcement
