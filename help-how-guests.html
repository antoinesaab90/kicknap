For Guests

How to find, book, and enjoy your perfect space

Getting Started

kicknap lets you rent spaces by the hour — whether you need a quiet desk, a meeting room, a creative studio, or just a place to nap. Here's how it works:

  1. Search — Enter your location and preferred time to find available spaces nearby
  2. Browse — View photos, descriptions, amenities, and reviews for each space
  3. Book — Choose your start and end time, then confirm your booking
  4. Arrive — Follow the Host's check-in instructions when your booking starts
  5. Enjoy — Use the space for your booked hours
  6. Leave — Check out at your end time and leave the space as you found it

How Booking Works

Booking Types

  • Instant Book — Book immediately, no approval needed from the Host
  • Request to Book — Send a request to the Host, who must approve before your booking is confirmed

Check-in and Check-out

Each space has specific check-in and check-out times set by the Host. These are displayed on the Listing page. You receive the exact address and access details when your booking starts.

What's Included

Everything listed in the space's description is included in your booking. This may include WiFi, desks, chairs, kitchen access, or other amenities. If something isn't listed, don't assume it's included.

Rules and Expectations

  • Arrive and leave at your booked times
  • Use the space only for the purpose described in the listing
  • Leave the space in the same general condition you found it (normal use is expected — you don't need to clean, but don't leave an extraordinary mess)
  • Respect the Host's house rules as displayed on the listing
  • Don't bring more people than stated in your booking

Payments and Fees

  • You pay the hourly rate set by the Host, plus a kicknap service fee (5-10%)
  • Payment is processed at the time of booking
  • A security deposit pre-authorization (€50-100) may be placed on your card
  • The security deposit is released within 7 days if no damage is reported
  • All payments are processed securely through Stripe

See our Payments Terms and Service Fees Policy for details.

Cancellations

Cancellation policies vary by listing. The available policies are:

  • Standard: Free cancellation up to 24 hours before check-in. 50% refund if cancelled within 24 hours.
  • Flexible: Free cancellation up to 1 hour before check-in.
  • Strict: 50% refund if cancelled up to 24 hours before check-in.

Check the listing's cancellation policy before booking. See our Cancellation Policy for full details.

Issues and Support

  • Problem with the space? — Contact the Host through kicknap messaging
  • Can't reach the Host? — Contact kicknap support at support@kicknap.com
  • Damage to the space? — Report it through the Resolution Center within 48 hours
  • Safety concern? — Contact local authorities first, then kicknap at safety@kicknap.com
