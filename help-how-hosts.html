For Hosts

How to list your space and start earning during your dead hours

Why Host on kicknap?

kicknap is built for people who have a space that sits empty for part of the day. You don't need to leave home. You don't need to change your lifestyle. You just list the hours your space is available, and earn money while you're at work, sleeping, or doing anything else.

Think of it as passive income. Your space earns while you don't.

Getting Started

  1. Create an account — Sign up at kicknap.com (free)
  2. Add your space — Photos, description, amenities, house rules
  3. Set availability — Choose which hours your space is available (e.g., Mon-Fri 8am-5pm)
  4. Set your price — Choose your hourly rate
  5. Choose a cancellation policy — Standard, Flexible, or Strict
  6. Publish — Your space goes live on kicknap

What Spaces Can I List?

Almost any space that can be used for a few hours. Popular categories include:

  • Desks and co-working spots
  • Meeting rooms
  • Creative studios (photo, music, art)
  • Napping spaces
  • Event spaces
  • Kitchen or cooking spaces
  • Outdoor spaces

Your space must be clean, safe, and accurately described. See our Community Guidelines for requirements.

How You Get Paid

  • Guests pay upfront when they book
  • kicknap holds the payment in escrow until the booking starts
  • At the end of the business day after check-in, you receive your payout
  • kicknap deducts a 3% host service fee from your payout
  • Payouts are sent via Stripe Connect to your bank account

See our Payments Terms and Service Fees Policy for details.

Your Responsibilities

  • Accuracy — Your listing must accurately represent your space (photos, description, amenities)
  • Availability — Keep your calendar up-to-date. If you're not available, block those hours.
  • Communication — Respond to booking requests and messages promptly
  • Check-in/out — Provide clear instructions for how Guests access and exit your space
  • Cleanliness — Your space should be clean and ready for each Guest
  • Safety — Ensure your space meets basic safety standards (functioning locks, no hazards)

What You Don't Need to Do

  • You don't need to leave your home
  • You don't need to greet Guests in person (you can provide self-check-in)
  • You don't need to clean during or after the booking (Guests are expected to leave in the same condition)
  • You don't need to change your lifestyle in any way

Damage Protection

If a Guest damages your space, you can file a Damage Report through the Resolution Center within 48 hours of check-out. The Guest's security deposit (€50-100) covers initial damage. For larger damage, kicknap will work to collect the additional amount from the Guest.

See our Host Damage Protection and Resolution Center policies.

Check Your Local Rules

Before listing your space on kicknap, you must verify that your use is permitted under local law. Every city and country has different rules. kicknap does not verify your compliance — that is your responsibility.

What to check:

  • Permits and registrations — Does your city require a permit, license, or registration to rent out your space (even by the hour)?
  • Zoning and land use — Is your space zoned for the type of use you intend (commercial, coworking, short-term rental)?
  • Night limits — If you also offer overnight stays, some cities limit the number of nights per year (e.g., Amsterdam limits vacation rental to 30 nights/year, dropping to 15 in certain neighborhoods from April 2026)
  • Lease restrictions — Does your lease or rental agreement prohibit subletting or commercial use?
  • HOA / VvE rules — Does your homeowners' association restrict short-term or commercial rental?
  • Taxes — Are you required to collect and remit tourist tax, VAT (BTW), or other local taxes?
  • Guest registration — Some cities require you to register Guests with local police or authorities
  • Insurance — Does your insurance cover commercial use of your space?
  • Safety codes — Does your space meet local fire, health, and safety requirements?

Examples of local rules (not legal advice):

  • Amsterdam: Vacation rental (overnight) requires a Vergunning Vakantieverhuur. Max 30 nights/year, dropping to 15 in certain neighborhoods from April 1, 2026. Hourly rental may fall under different commercial regulations.
  • Barcelona: Tourist rental license required. Strict limits in certain districts.
  • Paris: Registration number required for short-term rental. 120-day annual limit.

These examples are for illustration only. Rules change frequently. Always check with your local municipality and consult a legal professional.

Legal and Regulatory

As a Host, you are responsible for:

  • Complying with all applicable local, regional, and national laws and regulations
  • Obtaining any required permits, licenses, or registrations before listing your space
  • Paying applicable taxes on your income (income tax, VAT/BTW, tourist tax)
  • Having adequate insurance for your space
  • Complying with your lease, mortgage, or HOA/VvE rules
  • Complying with our Host Terms of Service

kicknap does not verify host compliance with local laws. If we receive notice from authorities that your Listing may violate applicable law, we may restrict or remove your Listing without prior notice.
