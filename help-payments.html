Payments

How payments, fees, and payouts work on kicknap

For Guests

How You Pay

  • Payment is processed at the time of booking
  • You pay the Host's hourly rate, plus a kicknap service fee (5-10%)
  • A security deposit pre-authorization (€50-100) may be placed on your card
  • All payments are processed securely through Stripe

Accepted Payment Methods

  • Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
  • Debit cards
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay (where available)

Security Deposit

A pre-authorization is placed on your card before the booking starts. This is not a charge — it's a temporary hold. The hold is released within 7 days of check-out if no damage is reported. If damage is reported, the pre-authorization may be charged.

For Hosts

How You Get Paid

StepWhat Happens
Guest booksGuest pays kicknap. Payment held in escrow.
Booking startsGuest checks in. Payment remains in escrow.
End of business day after check-inPayout processed. kicknap deducts 3% host service fee. Remaining amount sent to your bank account via Stripe Connect.

Payout Timeline

  • Payouts are processed at the end of the business day after check-in
  • Funds typically arrive in your bank account within 1-2 business days after payout
  • Payouts are made in EUR (€)

Service Fee

kicknap deducts a 3% host service fee from each payout. This fee covers payment processing and platform costs. See our Service Fees Policy for details.

Refunds

Refunds are handled according to our Cancellation Policy. If you are entitled to a refund:

  • Refunds are processed back to your original payment method
  • Refunds typically appear within 5-10 business days
  • The guest service fee is non-refundable in most cases (see Cancellation Policy)

Security

  • All payments are processed through Stripe (PCI DSS Level 1 certified)
  • kicknap never stores your full card details
  • All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • We comply with PSD2 (Payment Services Directive 2) requirements
