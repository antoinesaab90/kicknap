1. Overview

kicknap provides a Host Damage Protection program that helps protect Hosts when a Guest causes damage to their space or belongings during a booking. This program uses the security deposit system and the Resolution Center to resolve damage claims.

Important: kicknap is not an insurance provider. This program is not insurance. It is a platform-based damage resolution mechanism that facilitates compensation through the security deposit system and, where applicable, additional collection efforts.

2. Coverage

2.1 What is Covered

The Host Damage Protection covers physical damage to the Host's property caused by a Guest during a confirmed booking, including:

  • Damage to furniture, fixtures, or appliances
  • Damage to walls, floors, doors, or windows
  • Damage to electronics or other items in the space
  • Excessive cleaning costs beyond normal use
  • Missing items that were present before the booking
  • Loss of booking income if the space becomes unavailable for subsequent bookings due to damage

2.2 Maximum Coverage

The maximum coverage per booking is equal to the security deposit pre-authorization amount (€50-100), plus any additional amounts kicknap is able to collect from the Guest through the Resolution Center or collection efforts.

2.3 What is NOT Covered

  • Normal wear and tear
  • Pre-existing damage
  • Damage caused by the Host's own negligence
  • Damage caused by pets (unless the Host explicitly allows pets in their Listing)
  • Theft of the Host's personal property (this is a matter for police report)
  • Damage to the Host's vehicle
  • Liability for Guest injuries (this is covered by the Host's own insurance)
  • Damage reported more than 48 hours after check-out
  • Fire, flood, or other catastrophic damage where the cause is undetermined or cannot be attributed to a specific Guest during a confirmed Booking
  • Damage to neighboring properties, buildings, common areas, or any property not owned by the Host
  • Bodily injury or death of any person (Guest, third party, or otherwise)
  • Damage exceeding the security deposit amount where the Guest is unable or unwilling to pay the excess — Hosts must rely on their own insurance for catastrophic losses
  • Intentional acts of destruction (these are matters for law enforcement)

2.4 Fire — Important Limitations

In the event of a fire at your Accommodation during a Booking:

  • If the cause of the fire can be attributed to a specific Guest during a confirmed Booking, kicknap will facilitate recovery of damages from that Guest through the Resolution Center and, if necessary, collection efforts, up to the security deposit amount and any additional amounts the Guest agrees to pay or is legally ordered to pay.
  • If the cause of the fire is undetermined, or cannot be attributed to a specific Guest during a confirmed Booking, kicknap's Host Damage Protection does not apply. You must rely on your own property insurance.
  • kicknap's Host Damage Protection does not cover damage to neighboring properties or common areas. If a Guest causes fire damage to neighboring properties, you (as the property owner) may be liable to neighbors under Dutch law (Art. 6:173 BW). You must rely on your own liability insurance (aansprakelijkheidsverzekering) for this.
  • kicknap's Host Damage Protection does not cover bodily injury or death. If a Guest causes injury to themselves, other Guests, or third parties, you may be liable. You must rely on your own liability insurance.
  • kicknap does not guarantee the ability of any Guest to pay for damages. In practice, Guests who cause catastrophic damage often cannot afford to pay for the full extent of the damage. You should not rely on kicknap's Host Damage Protection as a substitute for adequate insurance.

3. How to File a Claim

  1. Inspect your space within 48 hours of the Guest's check-out
  2. Document the damage with photographs
  3. File a Damage Report through the Resolution Center within 48 hours of check-out
  4. Provide evidence: photographs, written description, estimated cost of repair or replacement
  5. Wait for review: kicknap will review the claim and may request additional information

4. Claim Process

4.1 Guest Notification

The Guest will be notified of the Damage Report and given an opportunity to respond within 72 hours.

4.2 kicknap Review

kicknap will review the evidence provided by both parties and make a determination based on the preponderance of evidence.

4.3 Resolution Options

ScenarioResolution
Guest agrees to paySecurity deposit pre-authorization is charged. Amount paid to Host.
Guest disputes, claim validSecurity deposit charged. If excess, kicknap attempts collection.
Guest disputes, claim invalidSecurity deposit released. No charge to Guest.
Damage exceeds depositkicknap collects excess from Guest. If Guest fails to pay, collection agency referral.

4.4 Payment to Host

If the claim is approved, the Host receives payment within 5 business days of the decision.

5. Guest Obligations

As a Guest, you acknowledge that:

  • You are financially responsible for any damage you cause during a booking
  • A security deposit pre-authorization will be placed on your payment method
  • If you cause damage, the pre-authorization may be charged
  • If damage exceeds the pre-authorization, you are liable for the additional amount
  • Failure to pay may result in collection efforts and account suspension

6. Host Obligations

As a Host, you acknowledge that:

  • You must inspect your space within 48 hours of check-out
  • You must file Damage Reports within 48 hours of check-out with photographic evidence
  • You must cooperate fully with kicknap's review process
  • You must not file fraudulent Damage Reports
  • You are responsible for maintaining your own property insurance

7. Fraudulent Claims

Filing a fraudulent Damage Report (e.g., claiming damage that did not occur, or exaggerating damage) is a serious violation of these Terms. kicknap may:

  • Permanently ban the Host from the Platform
  • Recover any amounts paid out from the Host's future payouts
  • Report the matter to law enforcement if applicable

8. Limitation of Liability

kicknap's liability under this program is limited to facilitating the resolution of damage claims through the Resolution Center. kicknap is not liable for:

  • Damage amounts that exceed the Guest's ability to pay
  • Delays in the resolution process
  • Outcomes of the resolution process
  • Any indirect or consequential losses
  • Damage to neighboring properties, buildings, or common areas
  • Bodily injury or death of any person
  • Damage caused by events with undetermined causes
  • Damage caused by events outside of a confirmed Booking

This program does not replace the Host's own insurance. Hosts are required to maintain adequate property and liability insurance as a condition of using kicknap. kicknap's Host Damage Protection is a contractual guarantee, not insurance. It does not cover catastrophic losses, third-party claims, or damage exceeding the security deposit. Hosts who rely solely on kicknap's Host Damage Protection do so at their own risk.
