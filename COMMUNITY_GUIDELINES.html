Our Mission

kicknap is a community built on trust, respect, and safety. These guidelines help us maintain a platform where everyone — Hosts and Guests alike — feels welcome and secure. By using kicknap, you agree to follow these guidelines.

1. Be Respectful and Honest

  • Treat every interaction with respect and kindness
  • Be honest in your listings, reviews, and communications
  • Do not lie, misrepresent yourself, or pretend to be someone else
  • Communicate clearly and promptly through the Platform
  • Honor your commitments — if you make a booking, follow through

2. Keep Each Other Safe

  • Report any safety concerns to kicknap immediately
  • If you believe someone is in imminent danger, contact local authorities first
  • Do not engage in any activity that could put yourself or others at risk
  • Hosts must ensure their spaces meet basic safety standards (functioning locks, no hazards, smoke detectors where required)
  • Guests must treat the space and the Host's property with care

3. Respect Privacy

  • Do not share other Members' personal information outside of what is necessary for the booking
  • Do not use hidden cameras or recording devices in listed spaces
  • Do not access areas of the space that are not included in the booking
  • Do not use the Platform's messaging tools to send commercial messages without consent
  • Handle all personal data in compliance with our Privacy Policy and applicable law

4. No Discrimination

kicknap is committed to building a community where everyone feels welcome. We do not tolerate discrimination based on race, religion, national origin, ethnicity, disability, sex, gender identity, gender expression, sexual orientation, age, family status, political affiliation, or any other characteristic protected by law.

See our Nondiscrimination Policy for details.

5. Keep it Legal

  • Do not use kicknap for any illegal purpose
  • Do not offer or solicit illegal activities
  • Do not offer or facilitate prostitution or human trafficking
  • Do not use the Platform to organize unauthorized parties or events
  • Comply with all applicable local, national, and EU laws
  • Hosts are responsible for ensuring their listings comply with local regulations (permits, registrations, zoning, taxes)
  • Hosts must independently verify that their space is permitted for the intended use before listing on kicknap
  • kicknap does not verify host compliance with local laws and is not responsible for ensuring that any Listing is lawful

6. Protect the Platform

  • Do not attempt to circumvent the Platform's payment system (e.g., paying Hosts directly)
  • Do not use bots, crawlers, scrapers, or other automated tools to access the Platform
  • Do not attempt to hack, reverse engineer, or compromise the Platform's security
  • Do not create multiple accounts to manipulate reviews or bookings
  • Do not engage in any practices intended to manipulate search results
  • Do not use the kicknap name, logo, or branding without permission

7. Create Quality Listings

  • Use accurate photos that represent the current state of your space
  • Write honest descriptions that include any limitations or quirks
  • Set fair and transparent pricing
  • Keep your availability calendar up-to-date
  • Respond to booking requests promptly

8. Leave Honest Reviews

  • Base reviews on your actual experience
  • Be constructive and specific
  • Do not use reviews to retaliate against another Member
  • Do not use discriminatory, offensive, or defamatory language
  • See our Reviews Policy for details

9. Reporting Violations

If you believe a Member, Listing, or Content poses an imminent risk of harm to a person or property, you should immediately contact local authorities before contacting kicknap.

To report a violation of these Guidelines, contact support@kicknap.com.

Except as required by law, kicknap is not obligated to take action in response to any report. However, we take all reports seriously and will investigate.

10. Enforcement

Violations of these Guidelines may result in:

  • Removal of content (Listings, reviews, messages)
  • Written warning
  • Suspension of account
  • Permanent ban from the Platform
  • Referral to law enforcement where applicable
