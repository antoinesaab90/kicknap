Overview

When you book a space on kicknap, you are agreeing to the Host's cancellation policy that applies to that Reservation. The applicable cancellation policy is displayed on the Listing page before booking. This page describes kicknap's standard cancellation policy and the different policies that may apply.

Service Fees

kicknap's guest service fee is non-refundable in all cases except when the Host cancels the booking or kicknap cancels due to a Major Disruptive Event.

Host Cancellations

If a Host cancels a confirmed booking, the Guest receives a full refund including the guest service fee. kicknap may also assist with rebooking an alternative space.

Repeat cancellations (3+ within 90 days) may result in account suspension or permanent ban.

Major Disruptive Events

If something outside your control forces you to cancel a Reservation (e.g., natural disaster, pandemic, government restrictions, closure of transport infrastructure), you may be eligible for a partial or full refund under our Major Disruptive Events Policy.

A Major Disruptive Event is defined as an event that: (i) is beyond the reasonable control of either party, (ii) makes it impractical or illegal to complete the Reservation, and (iii) was not reasonably foreseeable at the time of booking.

Booking Modifications

Guests and Hosts are responsible for any booking modifications they agree to make via the Platform. Modifications to extend a booking may result in additional charges. Modifications to shorten a booking are subject to the applicable cancellation policy for the portion being cancelled.

Reservation Issues

If you experience a Reservation Issue (e.g., the space is materially different from the listing description, the space is unsafe, or the Host fails to provide access), you may be eligible for rebooking assistance or a partial or full refund. Contact kicknap support within 24 hours of your check-in to report a Reservation Issue.
