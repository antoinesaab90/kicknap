Frequently Asked Questions

What is kicknap?

kicknap is a platform for renting spaces by the hour — desks, meeting rooms, studios, napping spaces, and more. It's like Airbnb, but for hours instead of nights. You can book a space for as little as one hour, or as long as a full work day.

How do I book a space?

Search for a space, choose your time, and book instantly. Payment is handled securely through kicknap. You'll receive the exact address and access details when your booking starts.

How much does it cost?

Prices vary by space and Host. kicknap charges a service fee of 5-10% on top of the hourly rate. There are no hidden fees.

How do I list my space?

Create a free account, add photos and a description, set your availability and pricing, and publish. It takes about 5 minutes.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. Payments are processed through Stripe, a globally trusted payment provider. Your card details are never stored on kicknap's servers.

What if something goes wrong?

Use our Resolution Center to report any issues. We'll review the case and help resolve it fairly.

Do I need a permit or license to list my space?

It depends on your city and country. Some jurisdictions require permits, licenses, or registrations for short-term or commercial rental — even by the hour. It is your responsibility as a Host to check your local rules before listing. kicknap does not verify compliance with local laws. See our For Hosts page for guidance on what to check.
