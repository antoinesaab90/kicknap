Cancellations

How to cancel and what happens with refunds

Guest Cancellations

You can cancel a booking from your kicknap account. The refund depends on the listing's cancellation policy and when you cancel.

Cancellation Policies

PolicyMore than 24h before check-inWithin 24h of check-in
Standard (default)Full refund50% refund
FlexibleFull refundFull refund up to 1 hour before
Strict50% refundNo refund

The cancellation policy for each listing is displayed on the Listing page before you book. Always check before confirming.

How to Cancel

  1. Go to your Bookings page
  2. Select the booking you want to cancel
  3. Click "Cancel Booking"
  4. Confirm the cancellation

Host Cancellations

Hosts may cancel a confirmed booking, but this is strongly discouraged as it disrupts the Guest's plans. Host cancellations may result in:

  • A full refund to the Guest (including service fees)
  • A penalty note on the Host's account
  • Reduced visibility in search results
  • Account suspension for repeated cancellations

No-Show

If a Guest does not show up for their booking without cancelling, the booking is considered completed and no refund is provided.

Refund Timeline

  • Refunds are processed back to your original payment method
  • Processing typically takes 5-10 business days
  • The guest service fee is non-refundable in most cancellation scenarios
  • The security deposit pre-authorization is released separately (within 7 days if no damage)

Disputes

If you believe a cancellation or refund was handled incorrectly, contact support@kicknap.com or use the Resolution Center.
