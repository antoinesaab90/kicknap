Trust & Verification

Trust mechanisms on the kicknap platform

Building Trust

kicknap connects people who may never have met before. The Platform provides several mechanisms that may help Members assess trustworthiness. These mechanisms do not guarantee the accuracy, reliability, or trustworthiness of any Member.

Identity Verification

All Members (Hosts and Guests) are required to verify their identity before using the Platform. This includes:

  • Email verification — Confirming a valid email address
  • Phone verification — Confirming a valid phone number via SMS
  • Government ID verification — Submitting a government-issued ID (passport, national ID card, or driver's license) for identity confirmation

Verified Members receive a verification badge on their profile. While we work to verify all Members, we cannot guarantee that all Members are verified.

Reviews

Reviews are one of the most important trust mechanisms on kicknap:

  • Only Members who have completed a booking can leave a review
  • Reviews are double-blind — neither party sees the other's review until both have submitted, or 14 days have passed
  • Reviews cannot be edited after submission
  • Both Guests and Hosts can leave reviews

See our Reviews Policy for details.

Security Deposits

Security deposits protect both Hosts and Guests:

  • A pre-authorization (€50-100) is placed on the Guest's payment method before the booking starts
  • If damage is reported, the deposit may be charged
  • If no damage is reported, the deposit is released within 7 days

Secure Payments

  • All payments are processed through Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor
  • kicknap never stores your full card details
  • Payments are encrypted end-to-end
  • Guests pay upfront; Hosts receive payouts after check-in

Host Damage Protection

If a Guest damages a Host's space, kicknap facilitates the resolution through the Resolution Center. See our Host Damage Protection terms.

Community Standards

All Members must comply with our Community Guidelines, Nondiscrimination Policy, and Content Policy. Violations may result in account suspension or permanent ban.
