Safety

Safety on kicknap

Important Notice

kicknap is an online platform that connects Hosts and Guests. kicknap does not control, inspect, verify, or guarantee the safety of any Accommodation listed on the Platform. kicknap does not perform background checks, criminal checks, or security screenings on any Member. The safety of any Accommodation is the sole responsibility of the Host. Your personal safety is your own responsibility. You should independently assess the safety and suitability of any Accommodation before making a Booking.

For Guests

Before You Book

  • Read the listing description carefully, including house rules and amenities
  • Check reviews from other Guests
  • Look at the Host's profile and verification status
  • Note the location and surrounding area

When You Arrive

  • Follow the Host's check-in instructions
  • Verify that the space matches the listing description
  • Note any pre-existing damage and report it
  • Ensure you know the exit routes
  • Check that locks function properly

During Your Stay

  • Use the space only as described in the booking
  • Don't allow unauthorized people into the space
  • If you feel unsafe, leave the space and contact kicknap support
Emergency: If you believe you or someone else is in imminent danger, contact local emergency services first (112 in the EU), then contact kicknap at safety@kicknap.com.

For Hosts

Space Requirements

  • Ensure your space has functioning locks
  • Remove or secure any hazards
  • Provide clear safety information (fire exits, emergency procedures)
  • Ensure smoke detectors are installed and working where required by law
  • Keep the space clean and sanitary

Managing Guests

  • Provide clear check-in and check-out instructions
  • Set appropriate house rules for your space
  • Be available (or designate someone) in case of emergency
  • Report any safety concerns to kicknap
Insurance Warning: Standard Dutch home insurance policies (opstalverzekering and inboedelverzekering) typically exclude commercial rental activity. Before listing your space on kicknap, you must verify with your insurer that your policy covers short-term rental use, or obtain a separate rental insurance policy (verhuurverzekering). kicknap's Host Damage Protection is not insurance and does not cover catastrophic losses (such as fire), damage to neighboring properties, or bodily injury. See Host Damage Protection Terms for details.

Fire Safety

For Hosts

Fire is one of the most serious risks associated with any space rental. As a Host, you are responsible for ensuring your space is safe:

  • Smoke detectors: Install smoke detectors where required by Dutch law and building codes. Test them regularly. kicknap may require smoke detectors as a listing standard.
  • Fire extinguisher: Consider providing a fire extinguisher, especially if your space has a kitchen or cooking facilities.
  • Fire exits: Ensure all Guests know the exit routes. Provide clear evacuation instructions.
  • Electrical safety: Ensure electrical installations comply with Dutch NEN standards. Do not allow Guests to use dangerous electrical equipment.
  • Heating and cooking: Ensure heating and cooking appliances are safe and in good working order. Never leave open flames unattended.
  • No smoking policy: Consider whether smoking is permitted in your space. If not, communicate this clearly in your house rules.

For Guests

  • Know the fire exit routes when you arrive
  • Do not tamper with smoke detectors or fire safety equipment
  • Do not leave open flames (candles, stoves) unattended
  • Do not overload electrical outlets
  • If a fire alarm sounds, evacuate immediately and call 112

What Happens If a Fire Occurs

If a fire occurs at a kicknap space during a Booking:

  1. Evacuate immediately. Your safety is the priority. Call 112 (EU emergency services).
  2. Contact kicknap at safety@kicknap.com as soon as it is safe to do so.
  3. File a report through the Resolution Center. kicknap will facilitate the damage resolution process.
  4. Contact your insurer. If you are the Host, contact your property and liability insurance provider immediately. If you are the Guest, contact your travel or personal liability insurance provider.
  5. Document everything. Take photographs and keep records of all communications.

Important: kicknap's Host Damage Protection covers damage to the Host's property caused by a Guest during a confirmed Booking, up to the security deposit amount. It does not cover: fire damage with undetermined cause, damage to neighboring properties, bodily injury, or damage exceeding the deposit amount. Hosts must rely on their own insurance for catastrophic losses. See Host Damage Protection Terms.

Platform Safety Measures

  • Secure payments — All payments processed through Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified provider
  • Security deposits — Pre-authorizations protect Hosts against damage
  • Reviews — Help the community identify trustworthy Members
  • Resolution Center — A process for handling issues quickly and fairly
  • Content moderation — Active monitoring for fraud, scams, and illegal content
  • Data protection — GDPR-compliant data handling (see Privacy Policy)
