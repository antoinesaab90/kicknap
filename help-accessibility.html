Accessibility

kicknap is for everyone

Our Commitment

kicknap is committed to ensuring that our Platform is accessible to all people, including those with disabilities. We strive to comply with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA and the European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882).

Platform Accessibility

We are working to ensure that kicknap is accessible through:

  • Semantic HTML — Proper heading structure and landmark regions for screen readers
  • Keyboard navigation — All functionality accessible via keyboard
  • Color contrast — Sufficient contrast ratios for text and interactive elements
  • Responsive design — Works on all screen sizes and devices
  • Alt text — Meaningful descriptions for images
  • Form labels — Clear labels for all form inputs
  • Error handling — Clear error messages and guidance

Space Accessibility

Hosts can indicate accessibility features in their listings, including:

  • Wheelchair accessible entrance
  • Step-free access to the space
  • Accessible bathroom
  • Elevator access
  • Wide doorways
  • Other accessibility features (as described by the Host)

When searching for spaces, Guests can filter by accessibility features to find spaces that meet their needs.

Known Limitations

As a young platform, we acknowledge that our accessibility is not yet perfect. We are actively working to identify and fix accessibility issues. If you encounter an accessibility barrier on kicknap, please contact us at accessibility@kicknap.com.

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on how to improve accessibility. Please contact us at:

Email: accessibility@kicknap.com

We commit to responding to accessibility feedback within 5 business days.

Legal Framework

Our accessibility efforts are guided by:

  • European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882)
  • Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA
  • Dutch Equal Treatment Act (Wet gelijke behandeling op grond van handicap of chronische ziekte)
  • UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities
