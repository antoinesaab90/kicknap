## Key Design Principles (Host)

1. **Onboarding in 3 steps** — Space → Price → Bank. Done in 5 minutes.
2. **"Zero lifestyle change" everywhere** — Every screen reinforces: list while at work, earn while you're away.
3. **Transparent pricing** — Host always sees exactly what they earn per booking.
4. **Payouts are automatic** — No manual withdrawal. Money arrives daily.
5. **Dashboard shows today first** — Host cares about "what's happening now" more than analytics.
6. **Accept/Decline is binary** — No complicated response system. Accept or decline, move on.
7. **Safety declarations are explicit** — "These are YOUR declarations. We do NOT verify." No ambiguity.
