Overview

kicknap charges service fees when a booking is confirmed. These fees help us run the Platform, provide 24/7 customer support, process payments securely, and invest in trust and safety features. We use a split fee model — both Guests and Hosts pay a portion of the service fee.

Guest Service Fee

When you book a space on kicknap, you pay a guest service fee. This fee is calculated as a percentage of the listing price and is displayed to you during checkout before you confirm your booking.

ComponentAmountPaid by
Guest service fee5-10% of listing priceGuest (at time of booking)

The guest service fee covers:

  • 24/7 customer support
  • Secure payment processing
  • Trust and safety features (ID verification, reviews, damage protection)
  • Platform development and maintenance

The guest service fee is non-refundable except as specified in our Cancellation Policy. If you cancel a booking, the guest service fee is not refunded. If the Host cancels, you receive a full refund including the guest service fee.

Host Service Fee

When a Guest books your space, kicknap deducts a host service fee from your payout. This fee is calculated as a percentage of the listing price.

ComponentAmountPaid by
Host service fee3% of listing priceHost (deducted from payout)

The host service fee covers:

  • Payment processing and payout administration
  • Platform tools for listing management
  • Guest discovery and marketing
  • Trust and safety infrastructure

No host service fee is charged on bookings that are cancelled by the Guest before check-in.

Taxes

Depending on applicable laws, VAT may be charged on service fees. The service fee shown to Hosts and Guests is VAT-inclusive where applicable.

Fee Changes

kicknap reserves the right to change service fees at any time. We will provide Members notice of any fee changes before they become effective. Fee changes will not affect bookings made prior to the effective date of the fee change.

Service Fee Refunds

The guest service fee is refundable if:

  • The Host cancels the booking
  • kicknap cancels the booking due to a Major Disruptive Event
  • The booking is cancelled within the applicable cancellation policy's full-refund window

In all other cases, the guest service fee is non-refundable.
