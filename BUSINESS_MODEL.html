01 Executive Summary

kicknap is a peer-to-peer marketplace for renting private spaces by the hour. Think Airbnb, but for daytime use — a few hours, a quarter day, a half day. Users book a room, apartment, or studio to rest, shower, change, work, or store luggage between destinations. Hosts earn money from their space during hours they're not using it.

The core insight: Airbnb hosts must leave their home to host someone. kicknap hosts don't. They list their space while they're at work, running errands, or simply not home. Zero lifestyle change. Passive income during dead hours.

Based in: Amsterdam, Netherlands
Legal structure: Operated under Learnix (IT services & consultancy). Will spin off into kicknap B.V. when revenue justifies it.

02 Problem & Opportunity

The Problem

Millions of people every day are in transition — between flights, between meetings, between hotel checkout and a late flight. They need a private space for a few hours, but:

  • Hotels charge full-night rates for a few hours of use
  • Coworking spaces offer desks, not privacy or rest
  • Airports have lounges but no beds, showers, or privacy
  • Airbnb only offers overnight stays
  • Cafés and lobbies are public — no privacy, no security, no comfort

Booking Variations: Instant Book vs Request to Book

The core tension: Guests want speed. Hosts want control. kicknap lets both sides choose what matters most to them — and the system enforces clear deadlines so nobody is left waiting.

Two Booking Modes

ModeHow It WorksWho ControlsSpeed
Instant Book (default) Guest pays immediately. No host approval needed. Host is notified after the fact. Guest Instant — booking confirmed in seconds
Request to Book Guest sends a request. Host has 2 hours to accept or decline. If no response, request is auto-declined. Guest pays only after host accepts. Host Up to 2 hours — host must respond within deadline

How Guests See It

Guest ActionWhat Happens
Guest searches for spacesFilter toggle: "All spaces" / "Instant Book only" / "Request to Book"
Guest picks a spaceBadge shows: "Instant Book" (green) or "Request to Book" (blue)
Guest selects time slotSees exactly which hours are available in the host's window
Guest confirmsInstant Book → pays now. Request to Book → sends request, waits for host.

How Hosts Choose

Host SettingDefaultWhat It Means
Booking modeInstant BookHost can change per listing. Instant Book gets more bookings.
Response window2 hoursOnly applies to Request to Book. Host must respond within 2 hours or request is auto-declined.

The Rule: Once You Pay, The Deal Is Final

TimingWho ControlsWhat Can Happen
Before guest booksHostAccept or decline (Request to Book only)
After guest books, before check-inBothGuest can cancel (refund rules apply). Host can cancel only for emergency (penalized).
After check-inNobodyBooking is locked. Guest stays until end time. Host cannot cancel or kick them out.
Why this matters: A guest pays for 2 hours, arrives, and starts using the space. The host cannot cancel, reduce time, or ask them to leave. The booking is a binding contract from the moment of check-in. This is the same protection Airbnb provides — and it's what makes guests trust the platform.

Minimum Booking Duration

No forced minimums. Hosts set their own minimum hours per listing. The pricing advisor suggests optimal minimums based on host goals. Some hosts want short bookings (2 hours), others want longer ones (4+ hours). The market decides.

Available window1-12 hours8 hoursTotal hours host makes available per day
Gap blockingAuto / ManualAutoIf gap between bookings is less than 1 hour, auto-block it (prevent awkward short gaps)

Trust & Safety

  • ID verification: Government ID + phone number required for both hosts and guests
  • Reviews: Two-way reviews after every booking. Mutual accountability.
  • Secure payments: Stripe escrow. Money held until 24h after check-in.
  • Security deposit: Pre-authorization on guest card. Charged only if damage reported.
  • Damage protection: Guest is financially responsible per Terms of Service
  • Theft liability: Guest is liable for missing items post-booking
  • Insurance: Basic business liability insurance (Phase 2). Full coverage program (Phase 3).
  • 24/7 support: Emergency contact line (Phase 2, initially email-only)
  • Background checks: Optional enhanced verification for premium hosts

09B Legal Protection & Liability

Platform Role (Critical)

kicknap is a marketplace, not a party to any rental agreement. We connect hosts and guests. We do not own, operate, or control any listed space. We are not a landlord, agent, or insurer. This distinction is essential for limiting platform liability.
