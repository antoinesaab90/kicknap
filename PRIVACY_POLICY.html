This Privacy Policy describes how kicknap ("we", "us", "our"), operated by Learnix, collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data when you use our platform, website, and services (collectively, the "Platform").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Dutch UAVG (Uitvoeringswet Algemene Verordening Gegevensbescherming).

We do not collect personal data that is excessive or unrelated to the provision of our Platform services. Where we offer optional features that require additional data, collection of such data is based on your explicit consent, and you may decline without affecting your ability to use the core Platform.
