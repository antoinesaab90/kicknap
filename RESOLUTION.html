Overview

The kicknap Resolution Center is where money-related disputes between Hosts and Guests are resolved. This policy describes how the Resolution Center works, how Damage Reports are handled, and how we make decisions.

1. What is a Damage Report

If a Host provides valid evidence that you, your guest(s), or your pet(s) have directly caused:

  • (i) physical loss or physical damage to the Host's real or personal property, or real or personal property the Host is responsible for, or has an economic interest in; or
  • (ii) loss of booking income for bookings via the Platform or other consequential damages which result directly from the damage caused under (i) above; or
  • (iii) the Host to incur cleaning costs in excess of the Host's standard cleaning fee

each of (i), (ii), and (iii) being a "Damage Report," the Host can notify kicknap and/or seek compensation through the Resolution Center.

2. Filing a Damage Report

2.1 Who Can File

Only Hosts may file Damage Reports against Guests. Guests may file reservation issue reports against Hosts through the same system.

2.2 Time Limit

Damage Reports must be filed within 48 hours of the Guest's check-out. Reports filed after this window will not be considered.

2.3 Required Evidence

The Host must provide:

  • Photographic evidence of the damage
  • A written description of what was damaged
  • An estimated cost of repair or replacement
  • Any other supporting documentation (invoices, quotes, etc.)

3. Guest Response

When a Damage Report is filed against you, you will be notified and given an opportunity to respond. You may:

  • Agree to pay — If you agree the Damage Report is valid, you authorize kicknap via kicknap Payments to collect the amount from your Payment Method
  • Dispute the claim — If you believe the Damage Report is invalid, you may provide counter-evidence and a written response
  • Propose a resolution — You may propose an alternative resolution to the Host through the Platform

4. kicknap Review Process

4.1 Initial Review

kicknap will review the Damage Report and any response provided. We may request additional information from both parties.

4.2 Decision

If kicknap determines, under consideration of the evidence provided and applicable statutory rules on the burden of proof, that the Guest is responsible for the Damage Report:

  • kicknap via kicknap Payments will pay out the Damage Report to the Host
  • kicknap may collect the amount from the Guest, including by charging the Guest's Payment Method up to a maximum of the security deposit pre-authorization amount (€50-100)

4.3 Burden of Proof

In any dispute resolution process, the burden of proof regarding the Damage Report and the Damage Report amount always lies with kicknap (acting on behalf of the Host).

4.4 Timeframe

kicknap aims to resolve all Damage Reports within 72 hours of receiving all required information from both parties.

4.5 Appeal

You may appeal a decision by contacting customer service. Any decisions made by kicknap do not affect your contractual and statutory rights. Your right to take legal action before a court of law remains unaffected.

5. Reservation Issues (Guest Claims Against Hosts)

If you experience a reservation issue (e.g., the space is materially different from the listing, the space is unsafe, or the Host fails to provide access), you may file a claim through the Resolution Center.

kicknap will review the claim and may:

  • Issue a partial or full refund to the Guest
  • Reduce the Host's payout
  • Cancel the booking and process a refund

6. Excess Damage Claims

If damage exceeds the security deposit pre-authorization amount:

  • kicknap will attempt to recover the excess amount from the Guest through the Platform
  • If the Guest fails to pay, kicknap may pursue collection through a collections agency
  • kicknap may also pursue legal remedies available under applicable law

kicknap is not responsible for recovering amounts beyond the security deposit pre-authorization unless we determine the claim is valid and the Guest is clearly responsible.

7. Good Faith Cooperation

All parties agree to cooperate in good faith, provide any information kicknap requests, execute documents, and take further reasonable action, in connection with Damage Reports, Member complaints, claims under insurance policies, or other claims related to the provision or use of services.

8. EU Complaint Handling (DSA)

For Members resident in the EEA, if kicknap takes any measures against you on the grounds that Content you provided is illegal or incompatible with the Terms, you may submit a complaint through our internal complaint handling system.

  • Complaints may be submitted for a period of six (6) months from the date you are informed about the measure
  • kicknap will provide a statement of reasons for any measure taken
  • You may also select a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body in accordance with Article 21 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065
