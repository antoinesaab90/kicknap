Overview

Reviews are a core part of the kicknap community. They help Hosts and Guests build trust and make informed decisions. This policy explains how reviews work, what is allowed, and what is not.

1. Who Can Leave Reviews

  • Both Guests and Hosts can leave reviews after a booking is completed
  • Reviews can only be left by Members who participated in the booking
  • You may only leave one review per booking

2. When Reviews Can Be Left

  • Reviews can be left after the booking's check-out time has passed
  • Both parties have 14 days to leave a review after check-out
  • Reviews are posted only after both parties have submitted, or after the 14-day window expires
  • This double-blind system prevents retaliatory reviews

3. What Reviews Should Include

Reviews should be:

  • Honest — based on your actual experience
  • Specific — describe what was good or bad about the experience
  • Constructive — help other Members make informed decisions
  • Respectful — avoid personal attacks or offensive language

Guest Reviews of Hosts

Guests may rate Hosts on:

  • Accuracy of the listing
  • Cleanliness
  • Communication
  • Check-in experience
  • Value for money
  • Overall experience (1-5 stars + written review)

Host Reviews of Guests

Hosts may rate Guests on:

  • Cleanliness
  • Respectfulness
  • Communication
  • Overall experience (1-5 stars + written review)

4. Prohibited Reviews

We may remove reviews that:

  • Contain discriminatory language or hate speech
  • Are defamatory, false, or misleading
  • Are retaliatory (left because the other party left a negative review)
  • Contain personal attacks, harassment, or threats
  • Are primarily promotional or contain spam
  • Violate our Content Policy
  • Were left for a booking that did not actually occur
  • Contain another person's personal information without consent

5. Review Transparency

kicknap does not verify reviews for accuracy. Reviews reflect the opinions of individual Members and may be incorrect or misleading. We encourage Members to read multiple reviews and consider the overall picture.

We do not:

  • Edit or alter the content of reviews (except to remove prohibited content)
  • Influence or manipulate review scores
  • Delete negative reviews simply because they are negative

6. Host Responses

  • Hosts may respond to Guest reviews
  • Responses must comply with this policy (no discriminatory, defamatory, or retaliatory content)
  • Responses are public and visible to all users

7. Review Removal

kicknap may remove a review if:

  • It violates this policy or our Content Policy
  • It is the subject of a valid legal request
  • kicknap determines it is fraudulent or fake
  • It contains information that is clearly inaccurate

If your review is removed, you will be notified with a statement of reasons (for EEA Members, in compliance with DSA Article 14).

8. Appeals

If you believe a review was wrongly removed, or if you believe a review about you violates this policy, you may contact support@kicknap.com to request a review.

For EEA Members: you may also use the internal complaint handling system described in our Terms of Service (Section 14) and, if not satisfied, select a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body.
