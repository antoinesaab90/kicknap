Our Commitment

kicknap is committed to building a global community where everyone feels welcome, regardless of race, religion, national origin, ethnicity, disability, sex, gender identity, gender expression, sexual orientation, age, family status, political affiliation, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law.

We believe that no one should face discrimination on kicknap. This policy applies to all aspects of the Platform, including Listings, bookings, reviews, communications, and interactions between Members.

Protected Characteristics

Under this policy, discrimination includes but is not limited to any adverse treatment based on:

  • Race, color, or ethnic origin
  • Religion or belief
  • National origin
  • Disability
  • Sex, gender identity, or gender expression
  • Sexual orientation
  • Age
  • Family or marital status
  • Pregnancy or parental status
  • Socioeconomic status
  • Citizenship or immigration status
  • Political affiliation
  • Criminal record or criminal background
  • Any other characteristic protected by applicable law

What We Prohibit

We prohibit discrimination in all forms on the Platform, including:

For Hosts

  • Refusing a booking based on a Guest's protected characteristic
  • Imposing different terms, conditions, or pricing based on a Guest's protected characteristic
  • Using language in Listings or communications that indicates a preference or limitation based on protected characteristics (e.g., "no [religion/ethnicity]", "ideal for couples only" when intended to exclude)
  • Canceling or declining a reservation based on a Guest's protected characteristic
  • Any other form of adverse treatment based on protected characteristics

For Guests

  • Discriminatory language in reviews or communications
  • Making booking requests with discriminatory intent
  • Any other form of adverse treatment based on protected characteristics

Exceptions

This policy does not prohibit:

  • Limited-functionality spaces — Hosts may accurately describe their space, including limitations (e.g., "ground-floor only, no wheelchair access"). This is not discrimination if it accurately reflects the space's physical characteristics.
  • Household rules — Hosts may set reasonable house rules that apply equally to all Guests (e.g., no smoking, no pets, quiet hours), provided these are not used as a pretext for discrimination.
  • Specific-purpose listings — Hosts may set legitimate requirements related to the nature of their service (e.g., a workspace listing may require guests to use the space for work purposes).
  • Legal obligations — Hosts may comply with applicable laws that may create distinctions based on protected characteristics.

Reporting Discrimination

If you believe you have been discriminated against on the Platform, please report it to us at support@kicknap.com. We take all reports seriously.

When reporting, please provide:

  • Your account email address
  • The Listing or booking in question
  • A description of the discriminatory conduct
  • Any supporting evidence (screenshots, messages, etc.)

We will not disclose your identity to the accused Member without your consent, except where required by law.

Investigation Process

Upon receiving a report of discrimination:

  1. We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 2 business days
  2. We will notify the accused Member and provide them an opportunity to respond within 5 business days
  3. kicknap will review all evidence from both parties
  4. kicknap will issue a written determination with reasons within 14 calendar days of receiving the report
  5. If the investigation is complex, kicknap may extend this period by up to 14 additional days and will notify both parties of the extension

Interim Measures

During the investigation, kicknap may impose interim measures including restricting the accused Member's ability to receive new bookings or cancelling pending bookings where there is an imminent risk of continued discrimination.

Enforcement

If a violation is confirmed, we may take any of the following actions:

  • Remove the offending content (Listings, reviews, messages)
  • Issue a written warning (first violation)
  • Suspend the Member's account (second violation)
  • Terminate the Member's account permanently (third violation or any severe/systematic discrimination)
  • Report the matter to relevant authorities where required by law

The determined Member may appeal through kicknap's complaint handling system described in Section 14 of the Terms of Service within 30 days of the determination.

Anti-Retaliation

kicknap prohibits retaliation against any person for reporting discrimination or participating in an investigation. If you experience retaliation (including but not limited to account suspension threats, negative reviews, or denial of service), please report it immediately. Retaliation will be treated as a separate violation of this policy.

EU Equal Treatment

For Members resident in the EEA, this policy is implemented in compliance with Council Directive 2000/43/EC (Racial Equality Directive), Council Directive 2000/78/EC (Employment Equality Directive), and all applicable national anti-discrimination legislation in the Netherlands, including the Algemene wet gelijke behandeling (General Equal Treatment Act).
